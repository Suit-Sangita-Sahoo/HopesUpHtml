Why You Should Master
The Skill of Being Present

Learning to be present can help you feel calmer, manage your emotions and make the most of the moment you are living right now.

June 15, 2026 • 7 min read

We tend to spend most of our day worrying about countless things, ruminating over past events and being anxious about what may happen in the future. We rush through our lives and forget to embrace the power of the present moment.

Becoming aware of the present moment brings a sense of calm and groundedness into your life. No matter what you do or where you go, the skill of being present will help you to live a more fulfilling life.

"The present moment is the only moment you can truly experience."

Here are some reasons why you should master the skill of being present in your life.

1. You Become Less Stressed

Many times, we end up losing our focus without even realizing it. One moment we are working, and the next we may be thinking about something that happened three months ago.

When you are completely aware of the present moment, you don't unnecessarily worry about past mistakes or feel anxious about the uncertain future.

Instead, you become more aware of what you need to do in the present moment to achieve your goals.

Being present helps you stop worrying and second-guessing your decisions. The more you think about thoughts from the past or the future, the more overwhelmed you may feel in the present.

Constantly worrying about what has already happened or what might happen can prevent you from focusing on your personal development.

REMEMBER

You cannot change the past or completely control the future. Bring your attention back to what you can do right now.

2. You Focus on the Things That Are in Your Control

This exact moment is in your control. Your actions and the decisions you make in the present are going to influence your life, not what you did in the past.

The past is gone. It is not in your control. The present, and the decisions you make in the present moment, are within your control.

So embrace the present moment completely. At this moment, focus on the things that need your immediate attention rather than worrying about things you cannot change instantly.

Pay attention to what is right in front of you and what you can control. Don't waste your time and energy on things that are not in your control.

Focus on one task at a time. Give your undivided attention to it. Be mindful of what is going on around you.

"Focus on what you can control, and let go of what you cannot."

3. You Manage Your Emotions in a Better Way

When you are aware of the present moment, you understand your thoughts and emotions in a better way.

Instead of getting stuck in emotional states, you acknowledge and accept your emotions.

You notice your negative emotions without judgment and allow them to pass instead of becoming consumed by them.

By strengthening your awareness, you become less restless and more capable of responding calmly to what is happening around you.

PRACTICE

Notice what you are feeling without judging yourself. Take a breath, observe the emotion and allow yourself to experience the moment.

4. You Become Able to Harness Opportunities as They Appear

When you are aware of the present moment, you see things as they are and learn to become more grateful for what you already have.

As a result, you develop a positive mindset, allowing you to harness valuable opportunities for your growth.

You become more spontaneous and are more willing to take risks, enabling you to break out of your comfort zone.

When your mind is constantly occupied with the past or future, you may overlook opportunities that are right in front of you.

Being present allows you to notice people, experiences and possibilities that you might otherwise miss.

"Bring your awareness to the present moment. This moment. This very moment."

Observe everything around you. Notice your surroundings, your thoughts, your emotions and the opportunities available to you right now.

BE PRESENT

Come Back
To This Moment

You don't have to solve everything at once. You don't have to know exactly what the future holds.

Give yourself permission to slow down, pay attention and experience the moment you are living right now.

The present moment is where your life is actually happening.
